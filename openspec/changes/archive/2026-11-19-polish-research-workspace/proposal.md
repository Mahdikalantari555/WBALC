## Why
Users need a clear next action after import, confidence in automatically proposed band names, and a way to recover work after refreshing.

## What Changes
Explain map action prerequisites, identify active imagery, distinguish inferred mappings from confirmed mappings, link imports to the exact raster on the map, and recover local class/band drafts.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `research-workspace-usability`: Import and map workflow guidance and recovery.

## Impact
Bilingual SvelteKit UI and browser-local metadata; no API migration.
